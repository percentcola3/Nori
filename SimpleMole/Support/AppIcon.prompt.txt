Nori — approved simplified ice-blue companion, September 2026.

Design: one gently tilted rounded pebble and two navy capsule eyes.
Flat fills: ice blue #BFEAF2, deep ocean #10283F. No text, gradients,
highlights, mouth, limbs, tools or baked-in lighting.

Production master: Nori/design.json, editable vector: Nori/Nori.svg.
Regenerate native geometry, SVG animations, PNGs and ICNS:
    bash script/make_nori.sh
Modern unmasked layers: Nori/AppIcon.icon (open in Apple Icon Composer).
The legacy ICNS is deliberately inset and pre-masked for macOS 13+.
Do not substitute this compatibility bitmap for the Icon Composer layers.
